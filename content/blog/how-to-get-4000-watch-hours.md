4,000 public watch hours in a year is the long-form route into the YouTube Partner Program. Breaking it down helps: that's an average of about **11 hours of watch time a day**. If 100 people each watch 7 minutes of your videos a day, you're there.

So the job is simple to describe: get the right people to click, and give them a reason to keep watching. Here's how.

## 1. Make every video for one audience

Channels that jump between unrelated topics confuse both viewers and YouTube's recommendations. Pick one audience and make every video for them. YouTube gets better at finding more people like them, and people who like one video are more likely to watch the next.

## 2. Win the first 15 seconds

Most viewers decide whether to stay almost immediately. Skip the long intro. Open with the payoff, the question the video answers, or the most interesting moment, then deliver on it.

## 3. Treat titles and thumbnails as one message

The thumbnail shows the moment and the title adds the curiosity. They shouldn't repeat each other. If a video's click-through rate is low in YouTube Studio, test a new thumbnail before giving up on the video.

## 4. Make longer videos people actually finish

Watch hours reward watch *time*, so longer videos can help, but only if people stay. A tight 12-minute video that holds attention beats a padded 25-minute one that viewers abandon.

## 5. Build series and playlists

"Part 1, Part 2" and well-organized playlists keep people watching the next video. One viewer watching three videos in a row is worth far more watch time than three viewers who each leave after one.

## 6. Use Shorts as trailers

Views in the Shorts feed don't count toward your 4,000 watch hours, but Shorts can bring in new viewers. Cut the best moment from a long video into a Short and point people to the full version.

## 7. Post on a schedule you can keep

A steady rhythm your audience can rely on beats a burst of uploads followed by weeks of silence.

## 8. Use end screens and pinned comments

Point viewers to your next-best video with an end screen, and pin a comment that starts a conversation. Both keep people on your channel.

## What never to do

These break YouTube's fake-engagement rules. The watch time gets removed, Partner Program applications get rejected, and channels can be terminated:

- Buying views, watch hours or subscribers
- Bots, view exchanges or looping your own videos
- Sub4sub groups or paying people to watch

Real viewers are the only watch hours that count, and the only ones that last.
