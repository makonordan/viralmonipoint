YouTube is raising the bar for creators who want to join the YouTube Partner Program (YPP). From **February 1, 2027**, new applicants will need twice the watch time or Shorts views they need today to unlock ad revenue.

If your channel is getting close to monetization, the next few months matter. Here's exactly what's changing, who it affects, and what to do about it.

## What's changing

For new applicants to the full Partner Program (the tier that includes ad revenue sharing), the thresholds change like this:

| Requirement | Today | From Feb 1, 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Watch hours (last 365 days) | 4,000 | **8,000** |
| Shorts views (last 90 days) | 10 million | **20 million** |
| Subscribers | 1,000 | 1,000 (no change) |

You still need 1,000 subscribers **plus** either the watch hours **or** the Shorts views, not both.

## Who it affects

- **Channels already in the Partner Program keep their status.** If you're already monetized, the new thresholds don't apply to you. YouTube does ask existing partners to accept the updated Partner Program terms in YouTube Studio by January 31, 2027.
- **Channels that aren't in yet** will need to meet the higher numbers if they apply after the change.

## Why timing matters

Meeting the thresholds makes you *eligible to apply*. It doesn't mean instant approval. After you apply, YouTube reviews your channel against its monetization policies, and that review can take weeks.

That's why waiting until late January is risky. A sensible target is to **reach the thresholds and apply before the end of December 2026**, so there's room for the review.

## What you can do now

1. **Check where you stand.** Our free [YPP Eligibility Checker](/tools/ypp-eligibility-checker) shows which tier you meet today and which metric is holding you back.
2. **Pick one path.** Decide whether long-form watch hours or Shorts views is the realistic route for your channel, and focus on it. Splitting your effort slows both.
3. **Focus on retention.** Watch hours come from people watching longer. Stronger openings, series and playlists help viewers keep watching.
4. **Avoid shortcuts.** Bought views, watch time or subscribers break YouTube's rules. They get removed, can lead to a rejected application, and put your channel at risk.

## The bottom line

The 1,000-subscriber requirement isn't moving, but the viewing requirement is doubling for new applicants. If you're within reach of 4,000 watch hours or 10 million Shorts views, now is the time to close the gap.
