"How do I get monetized on YouTube?" has two answers, because the YouTube Partner Program (YPP) has two entry levels. One unlocks fan funding. The other unlocks ad revenue. Here's how they compare.

## Tier 1: fan funding

To join the first tier, your channel needs:

- **500 subscribers**
- **3,000 public watch hours** in the last 365 days, **or 3 million public Shorts views** in the last 90 days
- **3 public uploads** in the last 90 days

Tier 1 unlocks ways for your audience to support you directly, such as channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks and YouTube Shopping.

## Tier 2: full Partner Program

To join the full program, your channel needs:

- **1,000 subscribers**
- **4,000 public watch hours** in the last 365 days, **or 10 million public Shorts views** in the last 90 days

Tier 2 includes everything in Tier 1, plus a share of the revenue from ads shown on your videos.

**Heads up:** from February 1, 2027, new applicants to the full program will need 8,000 watch hours or 20 million Shorts views. [Read what's changing](/blog/youtube-monetization-requirements-2027).

## Requirements on top of the numbers

Hitting the thresholds isn't the whole story. To be accepted, your channel also needs to:

- Follow YouTube's channel monetization policies
- Be based in a country or region where the Partner Program is available
- Have no active Community Guidelines strikes
- Have 2-Step Verification turned on for your Google account
- Have an AdSense account linked to receive payments

## Things that trip people up

- **Watch hours only count from public, long-form videos.** Views in the Shorts feed don't add to your watch hours, and private or deleted videos don't count.
- **It's watch hours *or* Shorts views.** You don't need both. Pick the path your channel is closest on.
- **Eligible isn't approved.** Meeting the numbers lets you apply. YouTube then reviews your channel, and that can take a few weeks.
