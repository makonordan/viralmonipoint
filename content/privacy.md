This policy explains what information ViralMoniPoint ("we", "us") collects when you use viralmonipoint.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

## Information you give us

**Contact form.** When you send the contact form, we receive your name, email address, phone number (if you add it), the package you're interested in, any referral code, and your message.

**Chat assistant.** The chat assistant on our site runs in your browser. Your questions and answers stay on your device unless you choose to send us your details at the end of the chat. If you do, we receive your name, email address, WhatsApp number (if you add it), channel link or name, the package you're interested in, your answers to the package questions, and any referral code.

**Partner program sign-up.** When you sign up as a partner, we receive your name, email address, WhatsApp number, the package you bought, where you plan to share your link, your preferred payout method, and the partner code created for you. We'll ask for payout details separately when a commission is due.

**WhatsApp.** If you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number and whatever you send us. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that conversation.

## Free tools

Our free creator tools at viralmonipoint.com/tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter into them is sent to us or stored by us.

## Information collected automatically

**Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use our site, such as which pages are visited, how long people stay, the type of device and browser, and the approximate location (country or city). Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. You can opt out with Google's [browser opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by blocking cookies in your browser.

**Server logs.** Our hosting provider, Netlify, automatically processes technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure.

**Browser storage.** If you arrive through a partner's link, your browser remembers their referral code so it can be included if you contact us later. If you sign up as a partner, your browser remembers your partner code so you can see it again. This information stays on your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

## How we use your information

We use your information to:

- reply to your enquiry and book your discovery call
- provide the services you buy from us
- credit and pay partner commissions
- understand and improve our website

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with

We only share your information with services that help us run the site and our business:

- **Resend**, which delivers form, chat and partner sign-up details to our inbox by email
- **Netlify**, which hosts our website
- **Google**, which provides Google Analytics

These providers may process information in countries other than your own. We may also share information if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and client information for as long as we need them to respond to you, provide our services, pay commissions and keep proper business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Contact us using the details below and we'll respond as soon as we can. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law.

## Children

Our services are for people aged 18 and over, because YouTube requires the person receiving monetization payments to be an adult. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us their information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Security

We use reputable providers and secure connections (HTTPS) to protect your information. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so please never send us passwords or login codes. We will never ask for them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
