FAQ

Questions people ask before starting

Do you guarantee my channel will get monetized? +

For Long-Form Monetization: we don't stop until you're there. If you're not YouTube Partner Program eligible by the end of the 3-month term (and you've held up your end), we keep working at no extra charge until you are. We can't promise a fixed number of days, though — YouTube's approval and audience growth aren't fully in our control, and anyone promising an exact day-count is either using bots against YouTube's rules or not being straight with you.

What's the difference between the three packages? +

Watch Hours Only is strategy and tutorials built to push an existing channel over the 4,000-hour line. Long-Form Monetization is full done-for-you production in a faceless AI niche over three months — 3 long-form videos a week, a daily 1-minute Short Monday through Saturday, all content creation, editing and posting handled for you, plus ongoing channel strategy and management toward YouTube Partner Program eligibility. Shorts Monetization is a one-time build of an automated Shorts system, run over a 3-month engagement paid upfront, aimed at the Shorts view threshold.

Do I keep ownership of my channel? +

Yes, always. We work as a manager on your YouTube Brand Account — your channel, your AdSense, your payouts.

How long does it usually take? +

It depends heavily on niche and consistency. Most channels take several months, not weeks — we'll give you an honest estimate on your discovery call, never a fixed promise. On Long-Form Monetization, our Work-Until-Eligible commitment means we don't stop until you hit YouTube Partner Program eligibility.

Can I combine or upgrade packages later? +

Yes. A lot of clients start with Watch Hours Only or Subscribers strategy, then move into full-service production once they see the plan working.
