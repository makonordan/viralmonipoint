Referral terms

Partner program terms

Plain-English rules so everyone knows how commissions work.

1. Who can join

The program is open to ViralMoniPoint clients aged 18 or over whose package payment has been confirmed. ViralMoniPoint may decline or remove any partner at its discretion.

2. How referrals are credited

  • A referral is credited when the new client arrives through your partner link or gives your code on the contact form, in the chat, or before their discovery call.
  • If a client gives more than one code, the first one recorded counts.
  • You can't refer yourself or a channel you own or manage.

3. Commission

  • 10% of the amount the referred client pays for their package, rising to 15% from your fourth paying referral onwards.
  • Commission is earned once the client's payment has cleared and 14 days have passed without a refund request. If a payment is refunded or reversed, the related commission is cancelled.
  • For the Long-Form plan, commission is earned on each monthly payment as it clears.

4. Payouts

  • Paid on the 5th of each month for commissions earned in the previous month, in USD or the local-currency equivalent, by bank transfer or mobile money.
  • The minimum payout is $20. Smaller balances roll over to the next month.
  • You're responsible for any taxes due on your commissions.

5. How to promote

  • Tell people you're a ViralMoniPoint partner and may earn a commission when you recommend it.
  • Never promise guaranteed monetization, approval by a date, or results ViralMoniPoint doesn't advertise.
  • Don't spam groups or strangers' inboxes, impersonate ViralMoniPoint, or run paid ads using the ViralMoniPoint name without written permission.
  • Never offer or promote sub4sub, paid subscribers, bots or view exchanges. They break YouTube's rules and put channels at risk.

6. Breaking the rules

Partners who break these terms may be removed from the program and lose any unpaid commission.

7. Changes

ViralMoniPoint may update these terms. Partners will be told in the Partners WhatsApp group at least 14 days before a change to commission rates takes effect. Commissions already earned are paid at the rate in place when they were earned.

Growth guide

10 ways to gain real subscribers

Share these with the creators you refer, or use them on your own channel. Real subscribers who watch are what YouTube rewards.

1

Pick one audience

Make every video for the same kind of viewer. A focused channel tells YouTube exactly who to recommend it to.

2

Hook in the first 15 seconds

Open with the payoff or the question, not an intro. Most viewers decide to stay or leave almost immediately.

3

Titles and thumbnails as a pair

The thumbnail shows the moment, the title adds the curiosity. Test a new thumbnail if click-through stays low.

4

Ask for the subscribe at the right time

Ask after you've delivered something useful, and give a reason: what they'll get from the next video.

5

Make series, not one-offs

Part 1, Part 2 and playlists keep people watching the next video, which builds watch hours and loyalty.

6

Use Shorts as trailers

Cut the best moment of a long video into a Short and point viewers to the full version.

7

Post on a schedule

A steady rhythm your audience can expect beats bursts of uploads followed by silence.

8

End screens and pinned comments

Send viewers to your next best video and pin a comment that starts a conversation.

9

Reply to comments early

Replying in the first hours after posting builds community and brings viewers back.

10

Read your analytics weekly

Check where viewers drop off and which videos bring in subscribers, then make more of what works.

What never to do

These break YouTube's fake-engagement rules. Subscribers get removed, Partner Program applications get rejected, and channels can be terminated:

  • Sub4sub or "subscribe to me and I'll subscribe back" groups
  • Buying subscribers, views or watch hours, or paying people to subscribe
  • Bots, view exchanges, or looping your own videos
